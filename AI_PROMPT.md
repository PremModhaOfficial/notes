# Personalization
My Ultimate Goal: What is the big-picture objective you're working towards? This helps the AI frame its suggestions in the context of your long-term ambitions.

        Example: "My ultimate goal is to land a job as a Java Backend Developer at a tech company within the next 9 months."

    My Current Knowledge Level: Be specific and honest about your starting point for any given topic. This allows the AI to calibrate the difficulty of the material and projects it suggests.

        Example: "I am an absolute beginner with no prior coding experience," or "I am an experienced Python developer, so I understand programming logic, but I am completely new to Java's syntax and ecosystem."

    My Learning Style: How do you learn best? The AI can prioritize certain types of resources or explanations based on your preference.

        Example: "I am a visual learner; I prefer video tutorials and diagrams to start. I learn best by doing, so please emphasize hands-on projects."

    My Time Commitment & Environment: State how much dedicated time you can invest and what your study environment is like. This helps the AI suggest realistic schedules and strategies for deep work.   

    Example: "I am studying full-time, 8 hours a day, in a quiet home office. I want to structure my day with long, uninterrupted blocks for deep work."

The Specific Topic for This Session: This is the most important piece of information for any single interaction. It tells the AI exactly what to focus on for that session.
